**Document Version:** 1.0
**Project Name:** Kaizen
**Document Type:** Product Roadmap
**Planning Horizon:** 3–5 Years
**Status:** Living Document

---

# 1. Executive Summary

This roadmap defines the planned evolution of Kaizen from an initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) into a comprehensive AI-powered health improvement ecosystem.

The roadmap prioritizes:

* Fast delivery of core value
* Strong technical foundations
* AI-first experiences
* Cross-platform support
* Long-term scalability

The roadmap is intentionally iterative and follows the Kaizen philosophy of continuous improvement.

# Roadmap Summary

Kaizen will evolve through a phased delivery model that prioritizes foundational health tracking capabilities, followed by AI-powered personalization, advanced analytics, social engagement, and ecosystem integrations. The roadmap is designed to balance rapid delivery with long-term scalability, ensuring the platform can grow from an MVP serving hundreds of users into a comprehensive AI-powered health ecosystem serving millions.
